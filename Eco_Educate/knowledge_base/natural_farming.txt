=== NATURAL & ORGANIC FARMING COMPLETE PRACTICES ===

1. WHAT IS NATURAL FARMING?
Natural Farming (also known as Zero Budget Natural Farming or Rishi Krishi) is an eco-friendly farming methodology where crops are grown without synthetic chemical pesticides, herbicides, or artificial fertilizers. It relies on natural biological processes, micro-organisms, cover cropping, and soil regeneration to nourish plants.

2. CORE PRINCIPLES OF NATURAL FARMING
- Zero External Chemical Inputs: Completely eliminates synthetic nitrogen/phosphorus fertilizers and chemical sprays.
- Soil Cover (Mulching): Keeps the soil covered with organic biomass (crop residues, dry leaves) to retain moisture, suppress weeds, and foster earthworm activity.
- Bio-stimulants & Microbial Inoculants: Uses traditional bio-formulations like Jeevamrutham and Beejamrutham to multiply beneficial soil microbes.
- Intercropping & Polyculture: Growing complementary crops together (e.g., legumes with cereal crops) to naturally fix atmospheric nitrogen and deter pests.
- Minimum Tillage: Preserves natural soil architecture, fungal networks (mycorrhizae), and prevents soil erosion.

3. KEY NATURAL PREPARATIONS & RECIPES
- Beejamrutham (Seed Treatment):
  * Ingredients: Fresh cow dung, cow urine, lime, virgin soil, water.
  * Usage: Coat seeds before sowing to protect against soil-borne and seed-borne fungal diseases.
- Jeevamrutham (Microbial Soil Tonic):
  * Ingredients: 10 kg fresh cow dung, 10 liters cow urine, 2 kg jaggery, 2 kg pulse flour (gram flour), handful of fertile soil, 200 liters water.
  * Process: Ferment for 48-72 hours in shade, stirring twice daily.
  * Application: Apply to soil near root zones or via drip irrigation every 15-20 days.
- Panchagavya (Organic Growth Promoter):
  * Ingredients: Cow dung, cow urine, milk, curd, ghee, banana, tender coconut water, jaggery.
  * Benefits: Enhances crop immunity, leaf size, flowering, and fruit yield.

4. NATURAL PEST & DISEASE CONTROL
- Neem Oil Spray: Mix 5ml pure neem oil + 2ml organic liquid soap per liter of water. Controls aphids, whiteflies, thrips, and caterpillars.
- Agniastra (Potent Botanical Insecticide):
  * Ingredients: Cow urine, crushed neem leaves, hot green chillies, garlic paste, tobacco leaves.
  * Usage: Boiled, filtered, and diluted in water to control severe stem borers and sucking pests.
- Brahmastra: Made with leaves of bitter medicinal plants (Neem, Custard apple, Papaya, Pomegranate leaves). Excellent general pest repellent.
- Yellow & Blue Sticky Traps: Placed in fields to trap flying pests like thrips and whiteflies naturally.

5. CROP-SPECIFIC PRACTICES
- Tomatoes (Growing Naturally):
  * Soil: Well-drained soil rich in organic compost; pH 6.0 - 6.8.
  * Companion Planting: Plant basil and marigolds nearby to repel tomato hornworms and nematodes.
  * Water Requirement: Deep watering twice a week at base level (avoid wetting foliage to prevent blight).
  * Organic Fertilizer: Apply Jeevamrutham every 14 days and vermicompost during flowering.
- Composting Techniques:
  * Layering 3:1 ratio of Brown biomass (dry leaves, straw) to Green biomass (vegetable scraps, fresh grass).
  * Maintain moisture like a wrung-out sponge; turn pile every 10 days for aerobic decomposition.

6. SEASONAL GUIDANCE
- Summer: Heavy mulching to prevent moisture evaporation, morning watering, shade nets for sensitive crops.
- Monsoon: Proper drainage channels to prevent waterlogging, spray Fermented Buttermilk solution to combat fungal mildew.
- Winter: Intercrop with legumes, apply organic compost to keep roots warm.

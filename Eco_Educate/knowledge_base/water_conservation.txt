=== WATER CONSERVATION & SUSTAINABLE WATER MANAGEMENT ===

1. THE WATER CRISIS & IMPORTANCE
While 71% of Earth's surface is covered in water, less than 1% is accessible fresh water suitable for human consumption and agriculture. Rapid population growth, pollution, climate change, and over-extraction of underground aquifers threaten global water security.

2. HOUSEHOLD CONSERVATION METHODS
- Low-Flow Fixtures: Install aerators on faucets and dual-flush toilets to save 30-50% water daily.
- Leak Repair: Fixing a single dripping tap can save over 3,000 gallons of water per year.
- Efficient Usage: Turn off tap while brushing teeth or shaving; run dishwashers and washing machines with full loads only.
- Greywater Recycling: Reuse water from washing machines and showers (greywater) for toilet flushing and garden irrigation.

3. RAINWATER HARVESTING (RWH)
- Catchment Systems: Collecting rainwater from rooftops into underground cisterns or storage tanks.
- Recharge Wells: Directing rooftop runoff into percolation pits to replenish dried underground aquifers.
- Benefits: Provides clean water for non-potable uses, reduces urban stormwater flooding, and prevents soil erosion.

4. AGRICULTURAL WATER MANAGEMENT
- Drip Irrigation: Delivers water directly to root zones, reducing evaporation loss by up to 80% compared to flood irrigation.
- Micro-Sprinklers: Efficient misting for high-density vegetable crops.
- Soil Mulching: Covering soil around plants reduces moisture evaporation dramatically.
- Drought-Tolerant Crops: Cultivating indigenous millets, legumes, and native varieties requiring minimal irrigation.

5. INDUSTRIAL & COMMUNITY WATER MANAGEMENT
- Wastewater Recycling: Advanced membrane bioreactors and reverse osmosis for zero liquid discharge.
- Wetland Preservation: Protecting natural swamps and mangroves that naturally filter pollutants and store floodwaters.
